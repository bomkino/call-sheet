# What to make next

A menu, not a plan. Each idea is a starting point. Bring a sharper one if you have it, and add the ones that work to `LOG.md`.

## For each list on the site

The site has ready-made lists: documentary, short film, feature film, TV series, animation, screenwriting, grants, labs, pitch forums, free to enter, and based in the US, UK, Canada or Australia. Each list can have a 10–15 s piece: its own revision colour, its three soonest opportunities and the loop on one. Pair each with that list's post.

## The carousels, in motion

The 11 team carousels (`../social/posts/v2/`) as 4:5 videos. Same covers, same calm. The slides turn as pages and the loop does the work the static red mark did. Good for LinkedIn and Facebook, where carousels underperform.

## pitch.dog's side

Call Sheet brings pitch.dog clients. These pieces bridge from finding an opportunity to pitching it.

| Piece | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| **Found it. Now the pitch.** | A Call Sheet card (WHAT TO SEND: pitch deck, treatment) slides aside, and a pitch deck's first page lands on the desk. "Applying for one? We make the decks that go with it." Ends on pitch.dog's mark, with Book a call at `cal.com/pitch.dog/wood` |
| **What to send** | For one opportunity, its WHAT TO SEND list ticks off item by item. The last item, the deck, is the one pitch.dog makes |
| **Behind the list** | How Call Sheet is made, in the same paper world: a page read with a red pen, a date checked against the official page, a corrected date. Calm and trustworthy, no process jargon |

Use only real quotes from real people, with their permission. Never invent a testimonial.
